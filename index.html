Projects

Wabash River: Coupled Hydrologic–Hydraulic–Sediment Modeling

Integrated HEC-HMS, HEC-SSP and HEC-RAS 2D with GPM-IMERG rainfall, observed streamflow, a 1 m DEM, LULC and soils. Calibrated and validated rainfall–runoff and MUSLE sediment models (2010–2024), ran flood frequency analysis and a 100-year flood with 2D sediment transport to locate erosion, deposition and flood hotspots.

HEC-HMS framework for the Swat River Basin testing lag-time modification across tributaries. De-synchronized flows reduced peaks by about 11.4% (upper), 16.5% (middle) and 26.8% (lower) basin sections.
